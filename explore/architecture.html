Architecture

Data and information architecture as a discipline; architecture principles and layers.

16 objects. Linked titles are published on this site; titles marked Professional or Architect belong to planned commercial tiers. Repository folder: 02-architecture

Concepts 3

  • Architecture DecisionA justified choice between alternatives that affects the structure, qualities or constraints of an architecture, recorded independently of the architecture documentation it shapes.
  • Data ArchitectureThe discipline and resulting structures that define how an organisation's data is described, organised, integrated, governed and realized across systems, from business meaning to t
  • Information ArchitectureThe structuring of information — its meaning, organisation, classification and relationships — so that people and processes can find, understand and use it consistently.

Principles 3

  • Decisions Belong to Their LayerEach architecture decision is taken at the layer where it belongs, by the role with decision rights for that layer, and lower layers do not silently override higher-layer decisions
  • Each Data Element Has an Authoritative SourceFor every shared data element, exactly one authoritative source is designated; other copies are derived and traceable to it.
  • Technology-Neutral Architecture FirstArchitecture is expressed in technology-neutral concepts and patterns first; technology-specific choices reference and realize them.

Assessment criteria 2

  • Architecture Layers Are Separated Professional
  • Authoritative Sources Are Designated Professional

Roles 5

  • Data ArchitectArchitect responsible for the realization of information in data structures, flows, history mechanisms and authoritative sources.
  • Domain ArchitectArchitect accountable for coherent architecture within one business domain, aligned with enterprise principles.
  • Enterprise ArchitectArchitect accountable for enterprise-wide principles, standards and cross-domain coherence.
  • Information ArchitectArchitect responsible for information models that represent governed meaning as paradigm-independent structures.
  • Solution ArchitectArchitect responsible for the design of a specific solution within enterprise and data architecture constraints.

Method 1

  • Layered Model DerivationDerive models layer by layer - governed concepts, then information model, then logical, then physical - keeping a traceability link at every step.

Capabilities 2

  • Architecture Decision Management CapabilityThe ability to take, record, govern and trace architecture decisions consistently across teams.
  • Historical Data Reconstruction CapabilityThe ability of an organisation to reconstruct past states of its data - as they were true and as they were known - for operations, audit and regulation.
