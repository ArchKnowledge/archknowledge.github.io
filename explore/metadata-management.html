Concepts 2

  • Data LineageMetadata describing the origin of data and the path, transformations and dependencies through which it flows from source to consumption.
  • MetadataData that describes other data or data-related assets — their meaning, structure, origin, quality, ownership, usage, security and processing.

Principle 1
