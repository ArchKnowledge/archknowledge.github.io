Semantic Architecture

Meaning: business concepts, ontologies, taxonomies, controlled vocabularies, RDF/OWL/SKOS.

13 objects. Linked titles are published on this site; titles marked Professional or Architect belong to planned commercial tiers. Repository folder: 04-semantic-architecture

Concepts 5

  • Business ConceptA unit of business meaning — a thing, event, role or idea the organisation talks and decides about — identified by a preferred term and a governed definition.
  • Controlled VocabularyA governed list of approved terms, each with a defined meaning, used to label and classify information consistently.
  • OntologyA formal, explicit specification of concepts in a domain, their properties and relationships, and logical axioms, expressed so that machines can reason over it.
  • Semantic ModelAn explicit, governed representation of meaning — concepts, their definitions, identity criteria and semantic relationships such as specialization, generalization, part-whole and e
  • TaxonomyA hierarchical classification of concepts organised by broader/narrower relationships.

Principle 1

  • Meaning Before RepresentationThe meaning of a concept is defined and governed before, and independently of, its representation in information and data models.

Pattern 1

  • Concept-to-Model TraceabilityLink every model element at each layer to the governed concept it represents and to the model element it realizes, so meaning and impact can be traced across layers.

Anti-pattern 1

  • Implicit SemanticsThe meaning of data exists only implicitly in column names, application code or people's heads, instead of in governed definitions.

Roles 3

  • Domain ExpertSubject-matter authority whose knowledge of the business domain informs definitions and models.
  • Ontology EngineerSpecialist who formalises semantic models as ontologies and ensures their logical consistency.
  • Semantic ArchitectArchitect responsible for the structure, formalism and integrity of semantic models across domains.
