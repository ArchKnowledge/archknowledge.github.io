Principles 2

  • Architecture Decisions Are TraceableSignificant architecture decisions are recorded with their context, options and rationale, and are traceable from source knowledge to implementation.
  • Governance Is Embedded in DeliveryGovernance controls are built into the delivery workflow as automated and review steps, rather than applied as a separate documentation phase at the end.

Anti-pattern 1

  • Governance as AfterthoughtGovernance is applied as a documentation and approval step at the end of delivery, instead of being embedded in the architecture and workflow.

Governance rules 3

  • Separate Decision per Governance LevelAn approval contains one decision for each governance level the object's layers require - semantic, information model, data architecture, technical - never a single combined approv
  • Separation of Duties in ApprovalNobody approves a change they proposed or an object they own.
  • Status Change Requires Governance RecordAn object may only be approved, published, implemented, deprecated or retired when an append-only governance record establishes that transition for its current version.
