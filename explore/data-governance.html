Data Governance

Ownership, stewardship, decision rights and policies for data.

29 objects. Linked titles are published on this site; titles marked Professional or Architect belong to planned commercial tiers. Repository folder: 06-data-governance

Concepts 3

  • Data GovernanceThe exercise of authority, control and shared decision-making over the management of data assets — who may decide what about data, on which basis, and how compliance is ensured.
  • Data OwnershipThe assignment of accountability for a defined set of data — its meaning, quality, access and lifecycle — to a business role with the authority to make decisions about it.
  • Data StewardshipThe operational care for data on behalf of its owner — maintaining definitions, metadata, quality rules and issue resolution.

Principle 1

  • Data Has an Accountable OwnerEvery governed data set, concept and architecture artifact has exactly one accountable owner with decision rights over it.

Assessment criterion 1

  • Data Ownership Is Assigned Professional

Roles 15

  • Architecture BoardGovernance body that approves architecture decisions above the agreed threshold and decides on exceptions and cross-domain conflicts.
  • Data ArchitectArchitect responsible for the realization of information in data structures, flows, history mechanisms and authoritative sources.
  • Data CustodianTechnical role operating the storage, security and availability of data according to owner decisions.
  • Data OwnerBusiness role accountable for a defined set of data: its meaning, quality requirements, access and lifecycle.
  • Data StewardOperational role caring for data on behalf of the owner: definitions, metadata, quality rules and issue resolution.
  • Domain ArchitectArchitect accountable for coherent architecture within one business domain, aligned with enterprise principles.
  • Domain ExpertSubject-matter authority whose knowledge of the business domain informs definitions and models.
  • Enterprise ArchitectArchitect accountable for enterprise-wide principles, standards and cross-domain coherence.
  • Information ArchitectArchitect responsible for information models that represent governed meaning as paradigm-independent structures.
  • Ontology EngineerSpecialist who formalises semantic models as ontologies and ensures their logical consistency.
  • Platform ArchitectArchitect responsible for data platform capabilities, technical standards and technology lifecycle.
  • Privacy SpecialistSpecialist who assesses personal data processing for privacy compliance and advises on privacy by design.
  • Security ArchitectArchitect responsible for security architecture, classification schemes and security controls for data.
  • Semantic ArchitectArchitect responsible for the structure, formalism and integrity of semantic models across domains.
  • Solution ArchitectArchitect responsible for the design of a specific solution within enterprise and data architecture constraints.
