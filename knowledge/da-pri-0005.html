Principle · Data Quality

Quality Is Managed at the Source

Data quality issues are prevented and corrected as close as possible to where data is created, rather than repaired downstream.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Statement

Data quality issues shall be prevented and corrected as close as possible to where data is created, rather than repaired downstream.

Rationale

Downstream repairs multiply (every consumer fixes the same problem differently) and hide root causes.

Implications

  • Quality rules are owned by the producing domain and enforced at entry or at the producer interface (Data Contract).
  • Downstream quality gates detect and report, and route issues back to the source.

Consequences

  • Positive: single fix, consistent data.
  • Negative: sources may be legacy or external systems that cannot be changed; then downstream correction is a documented, temporary exception.

Applicability

All shared data.

Exceptions

External data sources beyond organisational control; migration cleansing (Data Migration) as a one-time activity.
