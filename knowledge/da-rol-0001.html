Role · Data Governance

Data Owner

Business role accountable for a defined set of data: its meaning, quality requirements, access and lifecycle.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Purpose

Carry accountability for owned data so decisions about it have a legitimate decider (Data Has an Accountable Owner).

Responsibilities

  • Approve definitions of owned concepts.
  • Set quality requirements and accept residual quality risk.
  • Decide on access and sharing within policy.
  • Decide retention within legal limits, with the Privacy Specialist.

Decision Rights

Semantic level approver in the Decision Rights Model Architect: meaning, quality requirements, access. No technical design rights.

Inputs

Proposals from Data Steward; impact analyses; quality reports.

Outputs

Approved definitions; quality requirements; access decisions.

Required Competencies

Business domain authority; data literacy; ability to weigh risk.

Escalation Paths

Cross-domain conflicts → Architecture Board or data governance council.
