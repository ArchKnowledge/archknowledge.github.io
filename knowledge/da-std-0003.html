RDF 1.1

Resource Description Framework 1.1 — the W3C graph data model of subject–predicate–object triples identified by IRIs, underlying SKOS and OWL.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Scope

Abstract graph data model (triples, IRIs, literals, blank nodes, datasets) and serializations (e.g. Turtle, N-Triples, JSON-LD, RDF/XML).

Requirements

Data model definition; not a compliance standard.

Applicability

Semantic models, linked data, knowledge graph exports. This repository's dakb.py export emits Turtle as a transformation path.

Related Architecture Concepts

Ontology, Semantic Model, Metadata.

Related Controls

None.

Verification Notes

A successor (RDF 1.2) was in progress at W3C at the time of writing; status to be checked by the reviewer.
