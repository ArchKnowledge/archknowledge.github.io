Historical Data Reconstruction Capability

The ability of an organisation to reconstruct past states of its data - as they were true and as they were known - for operations, audit and regulation.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Description

Reconstructing past states on demand, with the time dimensions the business actually requires.

Outcomes

Reproducible reports and decisions; defensible audits; correct handling of backdated corrections.

Enablers

Historical Requirements Are Explicit, Database History Pattern Decision Tree, history patterns, platform support such as SQL Server Temporal Tables.

Maturity Indicators

Level Indicator
1 Fragmented History reconstructed ad hoc from backups and logs
2 Documented History requirements described per system
3 Structured History requirements in information models; patterns chosen with the decision tree
4 Governed History decisions recorded and approved; retention governed
5 Executable History requirements validated automatically against physical models
