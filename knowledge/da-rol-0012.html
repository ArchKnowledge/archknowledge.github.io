Role · Data Platforms

Platform Architect

Architect responsible for data platform capabilities, technical standards and technology lifecycle.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Purpose

Provide governed, fit-for-purpose platform capabilities.

Responsibilities

  • Define platform capability model and standards.
  • Select and lifecycle-manage technologies.
  • Approve physical/technical designs against standards.

Decision Rights

Technical/formal level approver for platform-related designs.

Inputs

Data architecture; technology market; operations feedback.

Outputs

Platform standards; technology decisions.

Required Competencies

Platform engineering; cloud; data technologies.

Escalation Paths

Architecture conflicts → Data Architect / Architecture Board.
