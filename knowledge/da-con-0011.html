Business Concept

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Definition

A Business Concept is a unit of business meaning — a thing, event, role or idea the organisation talks and decides about — identified by a preferred term and a governed definition.

Explanation

Business concepts are the anchor of the chain Business Meaning → Governed Concept → Semantic Model → Information Model → Logical → Physical. A business concept becomes a governed concept once its definition has an owner, a status and approval (see Terminology Governance and Governance Lifecycle).

A well-formed concept has: preferred term, definition, identity criterion ("when are two X the same X?"), synonyms, forbidden terms, broader/narrower concepts and an owner.

Distinctions

  • A concept is not a term: one concept can have several terms (synonyms), and one term can denote several concepts (homonyms). Governance manages the mapping.
  • A concept is not an entity: an entity in a Data Model represents one or more concepts.

Examples

  • "Customer", "Policy", "Claim", "Registration" in an insurance or public-sector context.
