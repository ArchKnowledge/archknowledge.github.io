Architecture Governance Model

The overall model of how data and architecture decisions are governed — layered decision rights, roles, lifecycle, controls and change process — treated as a first-class architectural layer.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Purpose

Define how governance works as an architectural layer: who decides what, at which layer, on which basis, with which evidence, and how decisions are recorded, published and changed.

Scope

Architecture and data governance for the organisation's data landscape, and — by application of the same model — this knowledge base (Knowledge Repository Governance).

The governance questions (spec §10)

Question Answered by
Who can decide? Decision Rights Model Architect, role objects
What can they decide? decision rights per layer
At which architectural layer? Architecture Layer
Based on which standards? standard objects, principles, constrained-by links
What evidence is required? controls (Evidence section)
Who approves? approver per layer in Decision Rights Model Architect
What is generated after approval? Architecture Change Process Architect step "Artifact Generation"
How is the decision recorded? ADR / governance decision objects, Git history
How can it be changed? Architecture Change Process Architect and lifecycle transitions

Principles

Governance Is Embedded in Delivery, Decisions Belong to Their Layer, Architecture Decisions Are Traceable, Data Has an Accountable Owner.
