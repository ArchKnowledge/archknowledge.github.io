Principle · Architecture Governance

Architecture Decisions Are Traceable

Significant architecture decisions are recorded with their context, options and rationale, and are traceable from source knowledge to implementation.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Statement

Significant architecture decisions shall be recorded with their context, options and rationale, and be traceable along the chain Source → Knowledge → Decision → Architecture → Implementation.

Rationale

Unrecorded decisions are re-debated, silently reversed, or cannot be defended to auditors.

Implications

  • Decisions use the ADR structure (see 00-system/templates/ADR Template.md).
  • Decisions link to the patterns and principles considered and to the architectures they affect.

Consequences

  • Positive: institutional memory, auditable architecture.
  • Negative: documentation effort; mitigated by lightweight ADRs.

Applicability

Decisions that are costly to reverse, cross team boundaries or affect governed data.

Exceptions

Easily reversible local decisions.
