Pattern · Data Integration

Batch Extract-Transform-Load

Periodically extract data sets from sources, transform them and load them into a target, in scheduled batches (ETL or ELT).

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Problem

Data from several sources must be combined and made available in a target on a predictable schedule.

Context

Analytical platforms, reporting, periodic interfaces, migrations.

Forces

  • Simplicity and robustness vs. latency.
  • Source load windows.

Solution

Scheduled jobs extract full or incremental data sets, transform them (before load: ETL; after load in the target: ELT), and load them into the target with restartable, idempotent steps.

Architecture

Sources → extract (full/incremental) → staging → transform → target → quality gate → publish

Advantages

  • Simple, mature, easy to reason about and reconcile.
  • Low runtime coupling.

Disadvantages

  • Latency equals schedule.
  • Incremental extraction without CDC may miss deletes.

Risks

  • Growing batch windows; silent partial loads without reconciliation.

When to Use

  • Latency of hours is acceptable; large set-based transformations.

When Not to Use

  • Near-real-time requirements → Change Data Capture.

Alternatives

Change Data Capture, Data Virtualization.

Examples

  • Nightly load of finance ledgers into a warehouse.
