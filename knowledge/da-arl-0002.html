Architecture rule · Decision Intelligence

Pattern Realizes a Concept or Principle

Every pattern realizes at least one concept or principle, keeping solutions traceable to the knowledge they serve.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Rule

For every object of type pattern: at least one realizes relationship to a concept or principle.

Rationale

Traceability from principle to pattern to decision (Architecture Decisions Are Traceable) breaks when a pattern floats free of what it serves.

Enforcement

Warning during validation (W100) and via dakb.py rules.

Exceptions

None known.
