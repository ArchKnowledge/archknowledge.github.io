Concept · Master Data

Master Data

The data about core business entities — such as parties, products, locations and assets — that is shared across processes and systems and provides context to transactions.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Definition

Master Data is the data about core business entities — parties, products, locations, assets — that is shared across processes and systems and provides context to transactions.

Explanation

Master data is characterised by: sharing across many processes, relatively slow change, high impact of errors, and identity problems (the same customer in five systems). Master data management (MDM) addresses identity resolution, consolidation into a Golden Record, distribution, and governance.

The architecture style (registry, consolidation, coexistence, centralized) is selected with Master Data Architecture Decision Tree Professional and realized by a Master Data Hub.

Distinctions

  • Master data vs Reference Data: reference data classifies (code lists: country, currency, status); master data describes business entities. Reference data is usually smaller, more stable, and often externally defined. The two are frequently confused; they are linked with distinct-from.
  • Master data vs transactional data: transactions (orders, claims) reference master data.

Examples

  • Customer, supplier, product, employee, site.
