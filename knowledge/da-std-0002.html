OWL 2

Web Ontology Language 2 — a W3C language for ontologies with formally defined semantics, enabling reasoning over classes, properties and individuals.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Scope

Formal ontologies: classes, object and data properties, individuals, axioms (subclass, equivalence, disjointness, cardinality restrictions), with profiles (EL, QL, RL) trading expressiveness for reasoning performance.

Requirements

Not a compliance standard; defines language syntax and semantics. OWL semantics are open-world: absence of a statement does not imply its negation.

Applicability

Domain ontologies requiring inference or consistency checking; semantic integration.

Related Architecture Concepts

Ontology, Semantic Model.

Related Controls

Model Validation Control Professional (ontology consistency checks).

Verification Notes

OWL 2 first became a Recommendation in 2009; the Second Edition date is from the author's knowledge; verify before approval.
