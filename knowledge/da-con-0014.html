Taxonomy

A hierarchical classification of concepts organised by broader/narrower relationships.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Definition

A Taxonomy is a hierarchical classification of concepts organised by broader/narrower relationships.

Explanation

Taxonomies support classification, navigation and aggregation (e.g. product categories, document types). In SKOS, a taxonomy is a concept scheme with skos:broader/skos:narrower relations. Many reference data sets are taxonomies.

Distinctions

  • Broader/narrower in a taxonomy is not necessarily class specialization ("is-a"): it may mean part-of or topical grouping. Ontologies make that distinction explicit.
  • Versus Controlled Vocabulary: a flat list without hierarchy.

Examples

  • Product category hierarchy: Insurance → Non-life → Motor → Motor third-party liability.
