Principle · Data Governance

Data Has an Accountable Owner

Every governed data set, concept and architecture artifact has exactly one accountable owner with decision rights over it.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Statement

Every governed data set, concept and architecture artifact shall have exactly one accountable owner with decision rights over it.

Rationale

Without an accountable owner, definitions drift, quality issues have no one to escalate to, and access decisions default to whoever administers the system.

Implications

  • Ownership is recorded as metadata and checked by the Ownership Control Professional.
  • Objects without owner cannot pass beyond proposed in the Governance Lifecycle (enforced by the validator for knowledge objects).
  • Owners must have authority matching their scope; see Decision Rights Model Architect.

Consequences

  • Positive: clear escalation, faster decisions, measurable accountability.
  • Negative: requires organisational commitment; nominal owners without time are a known failure mode.

Applicability

All shared and governed data; knowledge objects of this knowledge base.

Exceptions

Transient technical data (logs, temporary staging) may be owned collectively by the platform team. Exceptions are granted by the Data Governance body and recorded.
