Governance Roles

Overview of the reusable governance roles, their layer focus and their decision rights, linking to the individual role definitions.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Purpose

Provide a single overview of governance roles and their place in the Decision Rights Model Architect.

Scope

Domain governance roles. Repository roles (knowledge owner, reviewer, approver, publisher, maintainer) are defined in Knowledge Repository Governance.

Role Overview

Role Primary level Decides (summary)
Data Owner Semantic / business approves meaning, quality requirements, access for owned data
Domain Expert Semantic none formally; authoritative input on meaning
Data Steward Semantic / information proposes definitions, maintains rules and metadata
Semantic Architect Semantic semantic model structure, formalism
Ontology Engineer Semantic (formal) ontology axioms and formal consistency
Information Architect Information information model structure
Data Architect Data architecture logical structures, history, integration, authoritative sources
Domain Architect Data architecture (domain) architecture within a business domain
Enterprise Architect Cross-domain principles, standards, thresholds
Solution Architect Technical solution design within standards
Platform Architect Technical platform capabilities and technical standards
Security Architect Cross-layer security controls and classification rules
Privacy Specialist Cross-layer privacy assessment; advises on lawful processing
Data Custodian Technical operates storage and access per owner decisions
Architecture Board Data architecture (above threshold) approves significant decisions and exceptions

Separation of Duties

The approver of an artifact is not its author. One person may hold several roles in small organisations; the roles, and the recorded decisions, remain distinct.
