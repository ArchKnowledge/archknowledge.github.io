Technology-Neutral Architecture First

Architecture is expressed in technology-neutral concepts and patterns first; technology-specific choices reference and realize them.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Statement

Architecture shall be expressed in technology-neutral concepts and patterns first; technology-specific choices shall reference and realize them.

Rationale

Technologies change faster than business meaning and architectural intent. Neutral architecture survives product replacement and allows fair comparison of alternatives.

Implications

  • Every technology object links to the generic concept or pattern it implements.
  • Requirements are stated as capabilities before product selection.

Consequences

  • Positive: portability, clearer evaluation of products.
  • Negative: an extra abstraction step; risk of "neutral" designs that ignore real platform constraints.

Applicability

Conceptual through logical layers; platform capability models.

Exceptions

When a technology is a hard constraint (e.g. mandated by enterprise standard), record it as a constraint in the relevant Architecture Decision.
