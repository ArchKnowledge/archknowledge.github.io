Decision · Foundations

Declare Epistemic Status on Every Object

Every knowledge object declares epistemic_status (verified fact, external standard, common practice, expert interpretation, recommendation, hypothesis, experimental) and confidence, and leaves sources empty when no verified source exists.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Context

Spec §3.5 requires distinguishing facts, standards, practice, interpretation, recommendation, hypothesis and experiment. Spec §20/§31 forbid invented sources.

Problem

How to make uncertainty explicit and machine-checkable?

Options

  1. Object-level epistemic_status + confidence fields.
  2. Inline claim-level annotations only.
  3. Both.

Evaluation Criteria

Simplicity, machine-readability, granularity.

Trade-offs

  • Object-level is coarse: a pattern may mix common practice with recommendations. Claim-level is precise but heavy.

Decision

Proposed: option 1 now, claim-level annotation later where needed (e.g. "Open Questions" sections and explicit "flagged" statements in body text).

Consequences

  • In release 0.1, almost all objects have sources: [] and confidence reflecting the author's judgement. Standards objects carry a "Verification Notes" section: bibliographic details must be checked by a human before approval.

Assumptions

  • A human reviewer will attach verified sources during review.

Constraints

  • The LLM never invents sources.
