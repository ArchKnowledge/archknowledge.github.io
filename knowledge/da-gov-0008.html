Terminology Governance

How preferred terms, definitions, synonyms, forbidden terms and abbreviations are governed and how terminology inconsistencies are detected.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Purpose

Ensure consistent terminology across the knowledge base and the data landscape (spec §24).

Scope

Terms used in knowledge objects and in governed data definitions.

Term Record

Each governed term has: preferred term, definition (on the linked concept), synonyms, forbidden terms, abbreviations, related terms, domain, source, status. Machine-readable in 00-system/terminology/terminology.yaml; the definition lives in the linked concept object to avoid duplication.

Rules

  1. One preferred term per concept per language.
  2. Synonyms are stored as aliases on the concept (visible to Obsidian search and link suggestions).
  3. Forbidden terms must not appear in object bodies; the validator warns (W050-forbidden-term).
  4. The same label on two objects triggers a duplicate-concept warning (W011-label-collision).
  5. New terms follow the Governance Lifecycle; approval at the semantic level of the Decision Rights Model Architect.
