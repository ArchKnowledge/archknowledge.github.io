Role · Semantic Architecture

Semantic Architect

Architect responsible for the structure, formalism and integrity of semantic models across domains.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Purpose

Keep meaning explicit, consistent and reusable across domains (Meaning Before Representation).

Responsibilities

  • Design semantic model structure and conventions.
  • Select formalism (Select Semantic Modeling Formalism Professional).
  • Detect semantic overlaps, homonyms and conflicts.
  • Maintain concept-to-model traceability conventions.

Decision Rights

Semantic level: model structure and formalism; proposes definitions for owner approval.

Inputs

Business concepts; domain input; standards (SKOS, OWL 2).

Outputs

Semantic models; conventions; conflict reports.

Required Competencies

Semantic modeling; SKOS/OWL; facilitation.

Escalation Paths

Conflicting meanings across domains → Architecture Board.
