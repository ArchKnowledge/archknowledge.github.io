Method · Architecture

Layered Model Derivation

Derive models layer by layer - governed concepts, then information model, then logical, then physical - keeping a traceability link at every step.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Purpose

A repeatable way to go from business meaning to implementation without taking decisions at the wrong layer.

Steps

  1. Identify and govern the business concepts in scope (semantic layer).
  2. Represent them in an Information Model, stating history and quality requirements.
  3. Realize the information model in a Logical Data Model for the chosen paradigm; record paradigm and history decisions as ADRs.
  4. Realize the logical model in a Physical Data Model for the chosen store.
  5. At each step, link every element to the element it realizes (Concept-to-Model Traceability) and run model validation (Model Validation Control Professional).

Inputs

Governed concepts, requirements, principles, platform constraints.

Outputs

Information, logical and physical models with traceability links; ADRs per layer.

When to Use

Shared, governed or regulated data. For throw-away prototypes, steps may be compressed but concepts are backfilled.
