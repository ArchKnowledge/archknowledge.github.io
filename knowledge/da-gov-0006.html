Knowledge Quality Model

Quality criteria and quality statuses for knowledge objects, and how quality relates to lifecycle status, confidence and epistemic status.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Purpose

Make knowledge trustworthiness explicit and assessable. Machine-readable in 00-system/governance/quality-model.yaml.

Scope

All knowledge objects.

Quality Criteria

Criterion Question Automatable check
Accuracy Is it correct? — (review)
Completeness Are required sections and metadata present? yes: required fields/sections
Consistency Consistent terminology and relationships? partly: terminology, link resolution
Traceability Traceable to sources and related objects? partly: sources, relationships
Currency Up to date? yes: review_date
Applicability Context and limits stated? partly: "When not to use", "Exceptions" sections
Clarity Understandable? — (review)
Reusability Reusable across contexts? — (review)
Authority Backed by source or authorized approval? partly: sources, status
Confidence Declared confidence matches evidence? — (review)

Quality Statuses

Quality Meaning Compatible lifecycle status
draft unreviewed any
reviewed read by reviewer under-review or later
validated checked against criteria and sources validated or later
authoritative approved reference knowledge; not to be overwritten without explicit approval approved, published, implemented
deprecated no longer recommended deprecated, retired
superseded replaced; requires superseded-by deprecated, retired

Epistemic Status and Confidence

Every object also declares epistemic_status (verified-fact, external-standard, common-practice, expert-interpretation, recommendation, hypothesis, experimental) and confidence (high/medium/low). See Declare Epistemic Status on Every Object.
