Principle · Metadata Management

Metadata Is Captured by Design

Metadata is produced as part of designing and delivering data assets, preferably automatically, rather than documented afterwards.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Statement

Metadata shall be produced as part of designing and delivering data assets — preferably automatically — rather than documented afterwards.

Rationale

Metadata documented afterwards is incomplete and quickly outdated. Metadata created in the delivery flow is current by construction.

Implications

  • Definition of Done for data assets includes mandatory metadata (Mandatory Metadata Control Professional).
  • Technical and lineage metadata are harvested from tools (Metadata Harvesting); business metadata is captured in the modeling workflow.

Consequences

  • Positive: current catalog, reliable lineage.
  • Negative: tooling investment; requires metadata standards.

Applicability

All governed data assets.

Exceptions

Legacy assets are catalogued incrementally, prioritised by use and risk.
