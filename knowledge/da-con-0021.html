Concept · Data Quality

Data Quality

The degree to which data is fit for its intended uses, assessed against explicit requirements along defined quality dimensions.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Definition

Data Quality is the degree to which data is fit for its intended uses, assessed against explicit requirements along defined quality dimensions.

Explanation

Quality is relative to use: the same data can be adequate for trend reporting and inadequate for invoicing. Therefore quality requirements must be explicit, owned and measurable. Architecture contributes by: placing controls near the source (Quality Is Managed at the Source), building quality gates into flows, providing lineage for root-cause analysis, and designing models that prevent invalid states.

Relevant standards include ISO IEC 25012 Data Quality Model (a quality model for data) and ISO 8000 Data Quality (a series on data quality and master data exchange).

Distinctions

  • Data quality vs data validation: validation is one mechanism to check quality rules.
  • Data quality vs metadata quality: metadata has quality too (completeness of definitions, accuracy of lineage).

Examples

  • "99.5% of active policies have a valid postal code" as a measurable requirement.
