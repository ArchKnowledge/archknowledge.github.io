Decision · Foundations

Store Typed Relationships as Front-Matter Keys

Represent typed relationships as top-level front-matter keys named after controlled relationship types, each holding a list of wikilinks; store each edge once and derive inverses on export.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Context

Relationships must be explicit and typed (spec §6), machine-readable, validated, and visible in Obsidian's graph.

Problem

Where and how to store typed relationships?

Options

  1. Top-level keys per relationship type: realized-by: ["X"].
  2. A nested list: relationships: [{type: realized-by, target: "X"}].
  3. Inline in body text with a convention (e.g. realized-by:: X).

Evaluation Criteria

Obsidian graph visibility, validation simplicity, readability, portability.

Trade-offs

  • Option 1: links in list properties are shown in Obsidian's graph and properties panel; simple to validate; relationship names share the key namespace with metadata fields (validator rejects unknown keys).
  • Option 2: cleaner namespace, but nested links are not reliably shown in Obsidian's graph.
  • Option 3: depends on plugins (e.g. Dataview).

Decision

Proposed: option 1. Vocabulary in 00-system/relationships/relationship-types.yaml with declared inverses. Each edge is stored once on the source object; either direction of a pair may be authored.

Consequences

  • Validator rejects keys that are neither schema fields nor relationship types.
  • Body wikilinks remain untyped navigation, not relationships.

Assumptions

  • Obsidian version with property (front-matter) link support. To verify per installed version.

Constraints

  • Relationship names must be YAML-key safe (kebab-case).
