Concept · Data Modeling

Logical Data Model

A data model that structures information in a chosen data paradigm (e.g. relational, dimensional, graph, document) with keys, relationships and constraints, independent of a specific product.

Draft knowledge. Status proposed, quality draft. Not yet reviewed or approved by a human architect.

Definition

A Logical Data Model structures information in a chosen data paradigm — relational, dimensional, graph, document — with keys, relationships and constraints, independent of a specific database product.

Explanation

Decisions at this layer include normalization level, identifier strategy, the temporal modeling approach (e.g. Bitemporal Data Model versus Slowly Changing Dimension Type 2) and paradigm-specific structures. It realizes the information model and is realized by the Physical Data Model.

Distinctions

  • Not product-specific: data types are logical (e.g. "date", "amount"), not DATETIME2(7).
  • Not paradigm-independent: that is the Information Model.

Examples

  • A third-normal-form relational model of orders with surrogate and natural keys.
  • A dimensional model with a Sales fact and conformed Customer dimension.
