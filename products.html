How the tiers relate

Every knowledge object exists once and has one content tier: community, professional or architect. A product is a manifest — a view that sees some of these tiers and adds capabilities — not a copy of the knowledge and not a separate branch. Each product inherits everything from the one before it. Team and Enterprise add the customer's own extensions, which are kept separate from the shared graph.

Tier assignments are metadata in each object. They are currently proposals awaiting the owner's decision: 118 Community · 33 Professional · 4 Architect.

Commercial principles, in short

  • One authoritative knowledge graph; no copies per tier.
  • Open knowledge creates reach; value comes from putting knowledge to work.
  • Higher tiers add capability, not arbitrary paywalls.
  • Portable formats; no lock-in.
  • People, not AI, decide on approval and on commercial IP.
  • No invented customers, testimonials, prices or urgency.
