Roadmap

From open knowledge to architecture automation

Phases describe order and dependencies, not a schedule. No dates have been set.

  1. Phase 1

    Open Knowledge Graph

    In progress

    Governed open knowledge base, validator, exports and a first website generated from the graph.

  2. Phase 2

    Curated Professional Knowledge

    Planned

    Human-reviewed and approved content, professional-tier knowledge, public repository release.

  3. Phase 3

    Decision & Assessment Tools

    Planned

    Interactive decision trees and self-assessments generated from the same knowledge objects.

  4. Phase 4

    Team & Enterprise Governance

    Future

    Workspaces, customer extensions, governance workflows, audit and access management.

  5. Phase 5

    Architecture Intelligence Platform

    Future

    Search, API and graph store loaded from the repository; AI assistance that proposes but never approves.

  6. Phase 6

    Architecture Automation

    Future

    Architecture-as-code, validation of real architectures, artifact generation and change management.

Join the project

Get involved

The project is in phase 1: the knowledge graph is being built and reviewed. The source repository is private while licences and the first human review are settled. It is planned to open for contributions afterwards.

Contributions will follow a governed workflow: branch, change, automated validation, review, approval, merge.

Ways to help, once public

  • Review draft knowledge in your area of expertise.
  • Attach verified sources to existing objects.
  • Propose missing concepts, patterns or decision trees.
  • Flag contradictions and unclear definitions.
