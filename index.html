In five questions

An architecture knowledge graph for understanding, designing, governing — and eventually automating — architecture.

  1. What is this?

    Architecture knowledge stored as connected objects — concepts, principles, patterns, decisions, standards, governance — instead of documents. How the graph works

  2. Who is it for?

    Data, information and enterprise architects, data governance professionals, architecture teams and CDO/CTO organisations. Audience

  3. Why does it exist?

    Architecture knowledge is scattered across documents, diagrams, standards and people. Connecting it makes it reusable, traceable and governable. The problem

  4. What can I explore?

    118 open objects across data architecture, modeling, semantics, metadata, governance, quality and more. Explore domains

  5. What is coming next?

    Human review of the draft knowledge, curated professional content, then decision and assessment tools. Roadmap

Honest status

Where the project stands

  • All 155 objects are drafts: generated with AI assistance and awaiting review by a human architect. 0 are approved.
  • A validator checks every change against 76 rules for metadata, relationships, terminology and lifecycle.
  • The knowledge exports to JSON, CSV and RDF, and opens as an Obsidian vault.
  • The source repository is private during phase 1; a public release is planned.
  • No products are for sale. Paid tiers are ideas on the product page, marked “Coming later”.
